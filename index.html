About

I am a Ph.D. student in theoretical high-energy physics at Brown University, working with Profs. Marcus Spradlin and Anastasia Volovich.

My research focuses on modern on-shell methods in quantum field theory. In particular, I investigate the singularity structures of scattering amplitudes and cosmological wavefunctions. I also study the S-matrix bootstrap and its applications to effective field theories.

Before joining Brown in 2023, I received bachelor’s degrees in physics and electrical engineering from National Taiwan University.
